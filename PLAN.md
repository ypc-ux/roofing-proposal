## Financial Qualification Framework

### How We Verify Leads Can Pay

1. **Insurance Claims (Highest Priority)**
   - Ask during AI call: "Have you filed an insurance claim for this project?"
   - Storm damage leads are the #1 converter in roofing
   - Insurance pays 80-100% of replacement cost

2. **Home Equity Verification**
   - Use Audience Lab property data to verify ownership
   - Homeowners with equity can finance through HELOC or cash-out refi
   - Average home equity: $200K+

3. **Budget Confirmation**
   - AI asks: "Do you have a budget allocated for this project?"
   - Simple yes/no filter
   - Eliminates 60%+ of tire-kickers

4. **Financing Pre-Qualification**
   - Partner with home improvement lender (GreenSky, Mosaic)
   - "We offer financing options starting at $X/mo"
   - Expands your addressable market significantly

## Risk Reversal Strategy

### The Guarantee
- 20 qualified, booked appointments in 30 days
- If under 20, client only pays for what was delivered
- No gap charges, no questions asked

### Why This Works
- Shifts 100% of risk to you (the provider)
- Client has zero downside
- Creates trust and urgency
- If you deliver, they stay for years

### Churn Prevention
- Month 1: Over-deliver (aim for 25, deliver 20)
- Month 2: Show ROI data (X leads, Y closed, Z revenue)
- Month 3: They're hooked — raise to $250/lead if you want
